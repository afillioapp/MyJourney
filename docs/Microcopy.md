# My Journey: microcopy

Phase 2 deliverable (`Build-Plan.md:79`), owner copywriter. Written 2026-11-04. Revision round 2 of 3, 2026-11-05, after the rules audits `docs/reviews/2026-11-04-rules-art-microcopy.md` (FAIL, COPY-1 to COPY-9) and `docs/reviews/2026-11-05-rules-art-microcopy-r2.md` (FAIL, COPY-R2-1 to COPY-R2-3); not yet re-audited. It carries Spec §20 from A to DDDD, the latest ruling at this revision (ruled 2026-10-31), and Rubric R-182 to R-184 and R-187. Flows doc-conflict IDs are cited as DC-n, as Flows now numbers them (R-177). It fills every `[copy: copywriter]` marker in `Flows.md`, which was re-read on 2026-11-05 while under its own audit. `Flows.md` says how a person moves. This file says only what the words are. It holds no flows, screen structure, visuals, marketing-site copy or code.

**Sources.** All in `docs/`. *Spec* = `Functional-Spec.md`, by section, or by letter or number for §20. *Concept* = `My-Journey-Concept.md`. *Decisions* = `Decisions-Needed.md`. *Rubric* = `Rules-Rubric.md` (R-nnn). *SI* = `Screen-Inventory.md` (S-nn, OQ-n). *Flows* = `Flows.md` (FQ-n, DC-n). *Voice* = `CLAUDE.md`, "Voice". `Overview.md` is never cited. Questions raised here are MQ-n, so they do not collide with Decisions Q1 to Q5 (R-177).

**Labels.** `PROVISIONAL: <ID>` means the entry sits on a working default in Decisions. It is not a decision until the user says so. "Switchable" marks only the three defaults with a switch (B2, B4, B5; B11, B12 and C6 were fixed on 2026-11-10 by Spec §20 NNNN). An entry on a switchable default holds under both settings, and gives wording for both where they differ.

**How to read an entry.** One recommended line per marker. Text in curly braces is filled in by the app. Alternatives appear only for Commit, sealed and finishing the reveal, under the table of their flow. "Your Journey is ready" has none: see M-11. Each line has been read against R-182 and R-183: together with every other line placed in the same screen state, and in every state that reaches it. That means both settings of each switch, the clock at +5 years, a lost press, a lost page and a journey with no photos.
