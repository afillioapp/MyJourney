## The ritual in plain words

*A description for a new reader, not product copy.*

My Journey is a photo app with one unusual rule: you cannot see the photos you take until a year has passed. Not a preview, not a number. A year later the whole year opens at once, and you meet it. Using the app very little is the goal.

**You choose your year.** On any day you like, you choose one of three kinds of year: Explorer (4 photos a month), Wanderer (2 a month) or Contemplator (1 a month). Nothing is picked for you. Then you press Commit, and that word is meant. You cannot change your mind, you cannot stop early, and you cannot start another year until this one has opened. Say you choose Wanderer on 14 March. Your year runs until the next 14 March.

**You live it.** When a moment matters, you open the app and take the picture, in three taps. There is no second go and you do not get to look at it. Bad light stays bad. The moment it is taken it is sealed away, even from you. The app then offers one more thing, which you can skip: write something by hand on a sheet of paper and photograph it. That page becomes the writing on the back of the photo, as on an old print. You have up to 15 minutes, and for now just one go at it.

Photos you do not use in a month carry over to later months, and whatever is left when the year ends is gone. If you have none left, the app refuses the picture, without saying how many you have used. Depending on the kind of year you chose, you can also open a 24-hour window in which the monthly limit is lifted: four times for an Explorer, twice for a Wanderer, once for a Contemplator.

**You wait.** Open the app during that year and your year looks the same on every date. It shows what you chose, the day you started and the day it opens, always as plain dates. For now that is all it shows. No pictures, no count, no countdown. Nothing is wrong. That emptiness is what you committed to. The app never reminds you to take a photo or to come back. If you do come, what there is to do is read other people's years that have already opened. All that anyone else can tell about your year is that you have one under way.

**It opens.** On 14 March the whole year opens at once. Nothing pings you. For now, the plan is that a line tells you your journey is ready the next time you happen to open the app. This is the one real piece of work in the year. Writing is optional, and you may leave the whole thing unfinished. For now, nothing else is required either, not even a chapter, and there is no deadline. You read your photos and the pages you wrote. You can write a typed reflection on a photo. You name, by hand, who is in each picture: someone on the app, or a name you type for someone who is not. You give the year a chapter and whatever feelings fit. You can write how you changed across the year. And for each photo you decide who may read it: only you, your friends, or anyone using the app, which for now means anyone with an account. Until you decide, a photo stays open to your friends. Whether they can read it before you have finished is an option still being compared: for now they can, and the other option is that nobody but you reads your photos until you finish. Each choice can be made only once, and for now the app says so before it saves.

When you are done, you finish. Everything you chose locks. What adds a ring to the circle around your name is an option still being compared: for now, finishing adds one, and the other option adds one for each year with the app. For now, finishing is also when the year's line of activity is bent into the circle: a straight year turned into a circle. Rings are a record, not a score. Your friends get a one-line notice that your journey is out. A line, never a picture.

**Your map.** When you finish, the people you named who are on the app are placed on your map: you in the middle, with rings of people around you. A name you only typed in never counts. Someone in five or more of this year's photos sits closest, two to four in the middle ring, one in the outer ring. Someone already on your map who is not in this year's photos moves out a ring, but never falls off. Only you ever see your map. Nobody is told which ring they are on.

**Your friends.** Your map and your friends are separate. Two people become friends only when one sends a friend request and the other accepts it. A request waits quietly in a list inside the app: nothing pings you, nothing counts requests or friends, and the app never suggests people to add. Your friends can read what you share with your friends. Someone who is only on your map, because you named them in a photo, reads only what you share with everyone. For now, anyone can send a request to anyone, a decline is never shown to the person who sent it, either friend can quietly end the friendship at any time, and you can keep a list of close friends that only you see. What close friends get beyond other friends is still being decided: for now, nothing.

**The years after.** You can commit to your next year as soon as this one opens, or later. And every year on 14 March, each photo of that year makes room for one more reflection. You never change what you wrote. You add to it. Nothing tells you this has happened, and you never have to. Over time one photo becomes a stack: the moment, your handwriting from that day, then your typed voice from each year after.

Other people can walk your path. They read your years from the beginning to now, in order, seeing a hard time and then what you wrote about it later. Whether someone who is in one of your photos can find they were there in your hard year is an option still being compared. For now, they can. Readers can read. They can never write. The only voice on a photo is yours.

## Flow 1: Commit

**Who:** a signed-in user with no sealed journey. In the prototype, a fixture user picked in T-02 (`PROVISIONAL: C11`, `C16`).
**Starts:** Profile S-05, which holds the way to Commit only while the user has no sealed journey (Spec §20 C; S-05).
**Screens:** S-05, S-01, then S-03.

**Steps**

1. The user goes from S-05 to Commit S-01 on purpose. Nothing prompts them: no banner, no marker that Commit is open again (Spec §19; R-095, R-096).
2. S-01 shows the three types with difficulty, cadence, photos per year and highlights. Here these describe a choice, before any year is running (Spec §3; S-01). `[copy: copywriter]` *Type descriptions: describe how strict each year is, without making any type sound better or more worthy.*
3. The user picks one. No type is pre-selected (Spec §3). They can pick a different one until they press Commit, because only the press locks anything (Spec §3; Proposal:76).
4. The user presses Commit. `[copy: copywriter]` *The Commit moment and button: keep the word Commit (Spec §3; Concept:115) and make its weight felt: this year, this type, a journey that runs 12 months, no changing it, no stepping out. No relative form of the unlock date (Spec §20 EEEE). It must not sell or congratulate.*
5. The journey now exists, dated today, unlocking 12 months on (Spec §20 C). Type is fixed for that year (Spec §3). A journey always runs its full 12 months (Spec §20 3). A commit on the 29th to 31st uses the last day of shorter months, and the prototype uses one time zone (`PROVISIONAL: B16`).
6. The app goes to the sealed year S-03 (S-01). Continue at Flow 4.

**Branches and refusals**

- **No type picked.** There is nothing to commit. What the screen does if Commit is pressed anyway is not specified: FQ-2.
- **Confirmation.** The docs describe no second step before the press. Whether there is one: FQ-2.
- **Leaves without pressing.** No journey is created (Proposal:76). Nothing asks them back (Spec §19).
- **Already has a sealed journey.** Commit is not offered. A second Commit is refused at the data level, so no user-facing refusal exists because no route reaches it (Spec §20 C; Proposal check 3; S-01).
- **Between journeys.** Commit opens the moment the last journey unlocks, even with its reveal still open (Spec §20 C; S-01). The two run side by side: the old reveal stays available (`PROVISIONAL: B3`) while the new year is sealed.
- **Nobody is told.** No announcement exists for Commit (S-08; Spec §13). Others can learn only that a journey exists (Spec §20 I).

**Ends:** at S-03, with a sealed journey.

| Time state | Commit |
|---|---|
| `no-journey` · never committed | Open, on any day (Spec §3) |
| `no-journey` · between journeys | Open, the same, with no prompt |
| `sealed` | Not offered |
| Last journey `unlock-day` to `later-years` | Only as `no-journey` · between journeys (S-01) |

## Flow 2: Capture

**Who:** the owner of a sealed journey.
**Starts:** the sealed year S-03, or Start a highlight S-13 (S-02).
**Screens:** S-05, S-03, S-02, then S-12.

**Steps**

1. The app opens on S-05 (`PROVISIONAL: C12`). The owner goes to S-03.
2. On S-03 the owner goes to the camera. The way is offered the same whether or not a frame is left (`PROVISIONAL: B7`; R-138).
3. The camera S-02 opens: photos only, taken inside the app. No tagging, no writing, no choices (Spec §4). It shows nothing about frames (`PROVISIONAL: B7`). `[copy: copywriter]` *The no-retake line: state plainly that one press is one photo and the moment is gone. The copywriter decides where it sits (S-02).*
4. The owner presses the shutter. The press is judged at that instant, in this order: an open highlight that is not full, else a normal frame, else refused (Spec §20 U).
5. **Accepted:** the photo is sealed at once (Spec §4). It is never shown: no preview, no review before saving, no thumbnail, no retake (Spec §4, §6, §18; S-02). A bad photo stays (Spec §4). It can never be deleted (Spec §20 6).
6. The camera hands straight to the note step S-12 (`PROVISIONAL: C4`). Continue at Flow 3. That hand-over is the only sign a photo was taken. Nothing marks the time between press and answer (Spec §20 AAAA; R-170).
7. When the note step ends, the owner is back at the camera. They press again or leave to S-03.

The steps from app open to a taken photo are three presses: to S-03, to the camera, the shutter. These are Spec §4's "three taps" (decided 2026-11-10, Spec §20 OOOO).

**Branches and refusals**

- **Refused: no frame left, or the highlight is full and no normal frame is left** (Spec §20 1, 2, U). The refusal says capture was refused, with no number and no hint of when a frame returns (`PROVISIONAL: B7`; R-138). From there, back to the camera or out to S-03 (S-02). `[copy: copywriter]` *The capture refusal: say plainly this press was not taken. No number, no date, no encouragement, nothing that reads as a target.* There is no note offer after a refusal (S-12).
- **Frames over time.** Limits run per month of the journey, counted from the commit date. Unused frames carry forward and lapse at the end (Spec §20 1, 2). None of this is shown. Availability is learned only by trying (R-138).
- **During a highlight.** The camera looks the same and the note offer follows as usual. Highlight photos do not count against normal frames (Spec §20 5). The window closes at exactly its end, after 24 hours of elapsed time, or at unlock if sooner (`PROVISIONAL: B16`, `B25`; Proposal:133). Nothing changes on the camera when it does (R-140).
- **A bug the user cannot see, part one.** If the image fails the metadata guard, the app strips it again and saves once, at the press instant. The user sees nothing different (Spec §20 PP, VV, XX, AAA).
- **A bug the user cannot see, part two: a lost press.** If stripping fails twice, the press gives no photo, uses no frame and logs the bug. The screen looks exactly like the normal press it would have been, refusal or note offer included, and a note written for it is quietly discarded. Nothing invites a second press (Spec §20 DDD, GGG, JJJ, QQQ).

## Flow 9: Walking a path

**Who:** a reader: the owner, a friend or a public reader, or a tagged user reading as whichever of those they are. Someone on the owner's map who is not a friend reads as public (Spec §20 KKKK (d)). Being tagged gives no access (Proposal:293).
**Starts:** S-16 for another person, S-05 for one's own, or S-09's one photo (S-06; from S-08 only through S-16, Spec §20 RRRR). A path always opens at its beginning (`PROVISIONAL: B75`); from the feed the reader reaches the profile first (Spec §20 RRRR).
**Screens:** S-06, S-17, S-15.

**Steps**

1. The reader starts at the person's first journey and moves forward to now, in order, not across a grid (Spec §15). They move on purpose. Nothing scrolls on endlessly or plays by itself (Spec §13; R-104).
2. Within a journey they see the photos they are allowed to see, in order of capture date. A non-owner sees the date only, and same-day photos in a fixed order that carries no time (Spec §20 GG, JJ). The owner sees the full time (Spec §20 GG).
3. They read a hard time, and then the reflection written later on it (Spec §15). Reflections follow their photo's visibility (Spec §20 F). §20 EEE counts a reflection's date among the dates a reader can already see.
4. They can follow one photo forward on S-17: the original moment, its handwritten note on the back, then the stack of reflections in order (Spec §15; `PROVISIONAL: C13`). Then back to the path. A non-owner never sees when a note was written (Spec §20 LL).
5. Where they themselves appear: **never marked** (Spec §20 NNNN (b), 2026-11-10; B12 fixed to hidden). The path reads the same as for anyone with the same access. A reader may still recognise themselves in a photo they can read; the app adds nothing to that. *(Before 2026-11-10 a "shown" setting marked those photos in place: retired, with Microcopy M-25.)*
6. Owner and network can read the overview stack, back to back, on S-15 (Spec §9, §20 E).
7. The path reaches now. If the person's current journey is sealed, it shows as existing and nothing else (Spec §20 I). The path stops there, with no "more like this" or next person (R-104). The reader leaves.

**Branches and refusals**

| Reader | Photos | Type, dates, chapter, feeling tags | Overview |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | All, with full capture time | Yes | Yes |
| Friend (on the owner's map or not; close friends get nothing extra, `PROVISIONAL: B55`; Spec §20 KKKK) | Network and Public | Yes (Spec §20 T) | Yes |
| Public | Public only | Never. They may see the order of journeys, with no dates as fields (Spec §20 T, KKK) | Never |

- **Before the reveal is finished:** `PROVISIONAL: B2`, switchable. *From unlock (default):* the network reads every photo not yet set Private, and anyone reads photos already set Public. *Only after reveal finished:* no photo for anyone but the owner (Spec §20 R). See the time-state table.
- **Other people's tags:** never shown to a reader (Spec §20 NNNN (b), 2026-11-10; C6 fixed to hidden). SI OQ-20, whether a shown tag leads to a profile, no longer arises.
- **The owner on their own path.** On a photo with no reflection in the current slot, the owner can go on to Reflect, Flow 6. Nothing marks that a slot is open (`PROVISIONAL: C3`; R-095, R-096).
- **A journey or path with nothing this reader can see.** To a public reader the journey is not shown; to a friend it shows as a place with its type and dates only (`PROVISIONAL: B74`).
- **Writing.** No comment, reply or reaction exists (Spec §8, §18). Nobody but the owner writes.
- **From a Positivity Feed entry to a photo.** No next or previous control steps to another entry's photo (Spec §20 Q; R-145). Public-reader limits still apply there (Spec §20 T, CC).
- **Never, for a non-owner:** whether a photo was taken in a highlight (Spec §20 DD); the join date (Spec §20 TT). **Never, for any reader:** their own ring on this person's map (Spec §12, §18). With B12 fixed to hidden (Spec §20 NNNN), the reader has no tag to count; G-12's weakest point is gone.

**Ends:** when the reader leaves.

## Flow 10: Friends

**Who:** any user, on their own friendships. Each user sees only their own requests, friends and close friends (`PROVISIONAL: B56`; R-193).
**Starts:** another user's profile S-16, to send a request; the owner's own profile S-05, to answer, withdraw, remove or keep close friends.
**Screens:** S-16, S-05, S-22, S-23, S-24.
**The same in every journey state.** A friendship touches no journey, photo or note, and tells no one anything about a sealed year. A friend sees someone's sealed journey as existing and nothing else, like anyone (Spec §20 I; `Phase-1-Amendments.md` §12.5, §12.6).

### Part A: sending a request

1. The sender reaches the other person's profile S-16: by name (`PROVISIONAL: C10`; where the search sits is SI OQ-33), from an announcement, from the Positivity Feed, or from their own map. Nothing suggests whom to add (Spec §20 KKKK (c)).
2. On S-16 the sender presses the way to send a friend request (`PROVISIONAL: B43`). `[copy: copywriter]` *The friend request in each pair state: plain, one person to another. No count, no promise of what friendship gives, nothing that urges a reply.*
3. The request now waits in the other person's requests list S-22, and nowhere else: no notification, no dot, no count, nothing on any other screen (Spec §20 KKKK (c); `PROVISIONAL: B43`; R-194). The sender sees it as outgoing in their own S-22. S-16 shows only that the viewer's own request is pending (`PROVISIONAL: B61`).

**Branches and refusals**

- **Already friends, or a request of theirs already open.** A second request is not taken (`Phase-1-Amendments.md` §12.1).
- **The other person has already sent one.** S-16 looks exactly as if nothing waits (Spec §20 KKKK (c); R-194). Sending makes a crossed request, which is allowed, and accepting either makes them friends (`PROVISIONAL: B53`). S-16 never offers "accept" (`PROVISIONAL: B61`; settles DC-15).
- **Pending, declined or seen.** The sender can never tell (`PROVISIONAL: B44`; R-189).

### Part B: answering a request

**Who:** the person the request was sent to.

1. On S-05 the way into Friend requests S-22 is always there and looks the same whether or not anything waits (`PROVISIONAL: B43`; R-194). The person goes there on purpose. Nothing draws them (R-095).
2. S-22 lists incoming requests newest first, each by name, with no time (`PROVISIONAL: B52`), and offers accept or decline (`PROVISIONAL: B44`). `[copy: copywriter]` *Accept and decline: two plain answers of equal weight. Declining must read as an ordinary answer. No guilt, no count.* No line says an answer is final, and neither asks to confirm (`PROVISIONAL: B60`).
3. **Accept.** They are friends at once (Spec §20 KKKK (a)). From then each reads the other's Network photos, journeys and overviews, older ones included (`PROVISIONAL: B45`, "access follows the current friendship"; `Phase-1-Amendments.md` §13.5), and "journey launched" and "path crossed" reach them from then on (Spec §20 KKKK (e)). Neither sees the other's earlier announcements (`PROVISIONAL: B48`); whether they should is Decisions E3 (c), with the user. The sender is told by nothing: no notification (Spec §20 KKKK (c)), and no announcement, since the feed has only three events (Spec §13). The friendship simply appears in both friends lists S-23.
4. **Decline.** Silent. The request leaves the person's list and still looks pending to the sender (`PROVISIONAL: B44`; R-189). It is final for the one who declined, who may send a request of their own later (`PROVISIONAL: B50`).

**Branches and refusals**

- **No requests.** A plain statement (`PROVISIONAL: C9`). `[copy: copywriter]` *The empty requests list: a plain fact, no call to find people.*
- **The same person again and again.** A declined sender can withdraw and send again without limit, each time at the top of the list (`PROVISIONAL: B51`, `B52`). There is no block: Decisions E5, with the user.

### Part C: withdrawing a request

1. In S-22 the sender's own outgoing requests show, each with a way to withdraw (`PROVISIONAL: B44`). A declined one looks exactly like a pending one (R-189). `[copy: copywriter]` *How an outgoing request reads: the same words whether pending or declined. Nothing that implies the other person saw it.*
2. Withdrawn, it is gone for both. The sender may send again, with no limit (`PROVISIONAL: B51`). S-16 offers withdrawal too (`PROVISIONAL: B58`).

### Part D: removing a friend

1. In Friends S-23 (reached from the owner's profile S-05 by a way always present, with no count: `PROVISIONAL: B57`), each friend shows by name, A to Z (`PROVISIONAL: B59`), with no count, and a way to remove (`PROVISIONAL: B45`, `B46`). `[copy: copywriter]` *Removing a friend: say plainly what it does. No guilt, and no word that the other person will be told, since they will not be.* Removal asks once to confirm, with no "this is final" line (`PROVISIONAL: B60`).
2. Removal is silent and acts at once. The removed person reads only the owner's Public photos from then (Spec §20 KKKK (d); `PROVISIONAL: B45`; R-191), stops seeing the owner's announcements, and does not get them back by becoming friends again (`PROVISIONAL: B48`). Their place on the close-friends list ends and is not restored (`PROVISIONAL: B49`). Both maps stay as they were (B45). Nothing stores who removed whom (R-191).
3. Either of them may send a new request later (`PROVISIONAL: B43`, `B51`).

### Part E: keeping close friends

1. In Close friends S-24 (reached from S-23: `PROVISIONAL: B57`; how a friend is added is still open, SI OQ-55 (d)), listed A to Z (`PROVISIONAL: B59`), the owner adds any current friend or takes anyone off, at any time, silently (Spec §20 KKKK (f); `PROVISIONAL: B47`). `[copy: copywriter]` *What the close-friends list is: a list only you see. It must promise nothing close friends get, since nothing is chosen, and must not suggest anyone is told.*
2. Being on the list gives nothing yet (`PROVISIONAL: B55`). What close friends get is Decisions E2, with the user. Whatever is chosen will let a close friend work out that they are on the list (`Phase-1-Amendments.md` §12.6). The options named in E2 would reach Visibility S-20, Reflect S-14 and S-17, and Journey overview S-15. None is built.
3. No one but the owner sees the list or a count (`PROVISIONAL: B47`; R-193).

### Part F: blocking someone

1. On another user's profile S-16, friend or not, the owner chooses to block, and confirms once (`PROVISIONAL: B62`; Spec §20 MMMM). `[copy: copywriter]` *Blocking: say plainly what it does. No guilt, and no word that the other person will be told, since they will not be.*
2. At once and silently (`PROVISIONAL: B63`, `B64`): a friendship between them ends, as in part D, with the close-friends place; open requests between them close; and from then neither can find the other by name, open the other's profile, read anything of the other's, Public included, send a request, or see the other's announcements or Positivity Feed entries, earlier ones included.
3. The blocked person is never told. To them the owner looks like a name that does not exist (B64).
4. The rings do not change at the block. Earlier tags keep counting; since neither can tag the other as a user any more, each drifts outward as journeys pass with no crossing (`PROVISIONAL: B69`; Spec §20 G). **Whether a block should touch the rings is with the user: Decisions E6.**

### Part G: unblocking

1. From the owner's profile S-05, Blocked S-25 lists the people the owner has blocked, A to Z, with no time and no count (`PROVISIONAL: B65`, `B67`). Each has a way to unblock, confirmed once (`PROVISIONAL: B66`).
2. Unblocking is silent and restores nothing: no friendship, no close-friends place, no request, no earlier announcement (B66, B48). Either may then send a request (part A).

**Ends:** back at S-05 or S-16.

| Between two people | What each reads of the other |
|---|---|
| Not friends, whatever requests are open | Public photos only, as any public reader, map or no map (Spec §20 KKKK (d); `Phase-1-Amendments.md` §12.2, §12.4) |
| Friends | Network and Public photos, journey type, dates, chapter and feeling tags, overviews and reveal state, from the accept; announcements from the accept on (Spec §20 T, SS, KKKK; `PROVISIONAL: B48`) |
| A close friend | As any friend (`PROVISIONAL: B55`; Decisions E2) |
| After a removal | Public photos only. No earlier announcement returns, even after a new friendship (`PROVISIONAL: B45`, `B48`) |
| While either has blocked the other | Nothing at all, both ways, Public included; neither can find the other (`PROVISIONAL: B63`, `B64`; Spec §20 MMMM) |
| After an unblock | As two people who are not friends: Public photos only, and no earlier announcement (`PROVISIONAL: B66`) |

**Where E2 to E5 bite.** E2: part E and S-24. E3 (a), (b) and (c): **settled 2026-11-10 by Spec §20 NNNN** (unnamed, hidden, no), in Flow 5 step 6, Flow 7 part B step 3, Flow 9 step 5, part B step 3 and part D step 2.
